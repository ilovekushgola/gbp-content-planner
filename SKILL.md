# GBP Content Planner

Bulk Google Business Profile content planning and post generation for local businesses.

## Operating Sequence

UNDERSTAND -> RESEARCH -> PLAN -> APPROVE -> DRAFT -> HUMANIZE -> SEO CHECK -> CONVERSION CHECK -> VISUAL CONCEPT -> ASSET RECOMMENDATION -> AI IMAGE PROMPT -> FINAL QA

## Phase 1 - Business Discovery

Create a Business Knowledge Base: business identity, services, products, offers.

## Phase 2 - Business Asset Understanding

Identify brand, photography, trust, and digital assets.

## Phase 3 - Local City Audience Research

Research local customer needs, search behavior, seasonal demand.

## Phase 4 - Local SEO Keyword Research

Build keyword map: primary, secondary, long-tail, semantic.

## Phase 5 - Content Pillar Strategy

Select relevant pillars for the business.

## Phase 6 - Bulk Topic Planning

Create unique topics with varied pillars, intents, and local angles.

## Phase 7 - User Approval Gate

Stop. Get user approval before drafting.

## Phase 8 - GBP Post Drafting

Draft each post with topic, intent, keyword, local angle, copy, CTA.

## Phase 9 - Conversion Optimization

Ask: Why should customer care? Why this service? Why this business? Why now?

## Phase 10 - Humanizer Pass

Check specificity, locality, naturalness, rhythm, vocabulary, tone, repetition, authenticity.

## Phase 11 - SEO Pass

Verify keywords, entities, location, intent, no stuffing.

## Phase 12 - AI-Slop Final Audit

Scan for generic openings, AI vocabulary, puffery, formulaic patterns.

## Phase 13 - Visual Post Concept

Unique visual concept per post.

## Phase 14 - Asset Recommendation

Recommend real photos, brand assets, trust assets per post.

## Phase 15 - AI Image Generation Prompt

Production-ready prompt per post.

## Phase 16 - Bulk Quality Control

Batch-level audit for duplicates, distribution, variety.

## Phase 17 - Final Output

10-part package: understanding, insights, keywords, pillars, plan, posts, visuals, assets, prompts, QA.

## Hallucination Prevention

Never invent services, products, prices, offers, reviews, results. ASK -> VERIFY -> OR OMIT.
