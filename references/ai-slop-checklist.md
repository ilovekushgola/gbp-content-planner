# AI-Slop Avoidance Checklist

Use this checklist as a quality-control reference for every GBP post.

## A. Generic Introductions to Remove
Avoid: In today's fast-paced world, When it comes to, Looking for the best, Whether you're, We are thrilled to, Discover the power of, Unlock the potential of.

## B. Generic AI Vocabulary to Remove
elevate, unlock, unleash, transform, revolutionize, game-changing, cutting-edge, seamless, exceptional, unparalleled, remarkable, vibrant, dynamic, comprehensive, innovative, holistic, curated, empower, delve, journey, landscape, testament, pivotal, significant, crucial, fostering, enhancing, showcasing, ensuring, resonates.

## C. Superficial Analysis to Remove
Do not add: This reflects the changing needs, This highlights the importance, This represents a commitment to excellence.

## D. Promotional Puffery to Remove
Avoid: best in the city, number one, unmatched, world-class, premium experience, ultimate destination, trusted by everyone, industry-leading.

## E. Formulaic Patterns to Avoid
Not just X but Y, It's not X it's Y, Whether X Y or Z, From X to Y to Z.

## F. Natural Sentence Rhythm
Vary sentence length. Mix short, medium, and longer sentences.

## G. Natural Openings
Customer Problem, Local Context, Useful Tip, Specific Service, Question, Offer, Business Detail, Seasonal.

## H. Content Mix
Mix educational, commercial, trust-building, local, seasonal, behind-the-scenes.

## I. Final Slop Scan
No generic openings, no AI vocabulary, no empty claims, no repeated patterns, no keyword stuffing.
